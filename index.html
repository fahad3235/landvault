How LandVault verifies a plot

Deterministic checks on the record trail — no black box. Every point of risk is shown with its evidence.

1

Rebuild the chain

Ownership is traced across all four survey generations — CS, SA, RS, BS — plus every mutation. A break anywhere is flagged.

2

Verify the documents

Deed and khatian hashes are compared against the registry. A single altered character fails the check.

3

Score the risk

Six fraud signals are weighted into a 0–100 score with plain-language evidence and a buyer's checklist.
